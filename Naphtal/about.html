Background On Richfield Connect

Richfield Connect is a social media platform designed by a student for students to foster academic engagement, collaboration, and networking among students in a secure institutional environment.

Here on this platform, students can post thoughts, and comments and share content in a secure environment.

Richfield Connect web was created by Ramawa Naphtal Junior in the academic year of 2026.
